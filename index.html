03 How a project runs

You see working software early, and you know the price before each step.

  1. 01

    Frame the problem

    A call, then a short written brief: the outcome, who will use it, what is out of scope, and a fixed price for the next step.

  2. 02

    Prototype the path

    A clickable prototype or a working spike on your real data, so you can judge the direction before paying for the full build.

  3. 03

    Build and validate

    Working software every one to two weeks, tested against how your team actually works.

  4. 04

    Release and hand over

    Deployed, documented and yours: the code, the accounts and the know-how to run it.
